# iAqualink Heat Pump (Unofficial)

> ⚠️ **Unofficial.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Zodiac, Fluidra, iAquaLink, or Home Assistant.

Read-only Home Assistant integration for Zodiac / Fluidra heat pumps (`zs500`, `hpm`) via the iAquaLink cloud.

- UI setup with email + password
- Auto-discovers your heat pump
- Creates one sensor per shadow value (temperatures, pressures, modes, …)
- Cloud-polling every 60 seconds

After installing, go to **Settings → Devices & Services → Add integration → iAqualink Heat Pump** and sign in.
